Why crack hydrocarbons?

  • Supply meets demand: short-chain fuels such as petrol are in higher demand than long-chain hydrocarbons.
  • Alkenes are made: ethene is the starting material for plastics such as poly(ethene).
  • Shorter alkanes are more volatile and easier to ignite, so they make better fuels.
  • Hydrogen, when produced, is used to make chemicals such as ammonia.
